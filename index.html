The 50/30/20 Budget Rule — The Only Budget You'll Ever Need

📅 May 2026  ·  ⏱ 6 min read  ·  👁 12,400 views

The 50/30/20 rule is the simplest budgeting method for anyone starting from zero. Spend 50% on needs, 30% on wants, and put 20% toward savings and debt. This one rule can completely change your financial life without complicated spreadsheets.

Whether you earn Rs. 15,000 or Rs. 150,000 a month, this method scales perfectly and takes just 10 minutes to set up. We walk you through it step by step with real examples.
